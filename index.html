Research

I am a second year Ph.D. student in the ECE Department at The Ohio State University, advised by Professor Jia (Kevin) Liu. Before joining OSU, I majored in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics and received my Bachelor of Science from the School of Mathematics at Southeast University in 2024. I also studied at the University of California Santa Barbara as a visiting student in 2022.

My research focuses on optimization, reinforcement learning, and multi-armed bandits, with interests in theory-guided algorithms for sequential decision-making and networked intelligent systems.
